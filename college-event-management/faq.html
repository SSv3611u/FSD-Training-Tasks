Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our events

To register for events, simply navigate to the Registration page from our website. Fill in your details including name, roll number, email, and select the events you wish to participate in. Once you submit the form, you'll receive a confirmation message.

Most of our events are free for registered college students. However, some specialized workshops or guest lectures may have a nominal fee. Details about fees, if any, will be clearly mentioned on the event description page.

Yes! You can register for multiple events during the registration process. However, please ensure that the event timings don't conflict. Check the event schedule to avoid any clashes with other registered events.

The registration deadline varies for each event. Typically, registration closes 2-3 days before the event date. You can check the specific deadline on the event details page or by contacting our support team.

Yes, certain events like Hackathons, Coding Contests, and Group Projects require team participation. When registering, specify your team size and provide details of your team members if required. Maximum team size depends on the specific event.

You can retrieve your registration details by visiting our support page or contacting the event management team at events@college.edu. They will verify your identity using your email and contact details to provide you with your registration information.

Yes, you can cancel your registration up to 3 days before the event. Simply contact the event management team with your registration details. Cancellations made after this period cannot be accepted.
